# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, constructive project for everyone. Be respectful of differing experience and perspectives. Assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreements on ideas rather than people.

Harassment, hate speech, threats, sharing someone else's private information (including credentials), and repeated unwanted contact are not welcome in project spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation as needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the owner's profile rather than posting sensitive details publicly. Maintainers will review reports confidentially to the extent possible and respond proportionately.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces.
