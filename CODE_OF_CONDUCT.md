# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, constructive project for everyone. Be respectful of differing experience and perspectives. Assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreements on ideas rather than people.

Harassment, hate speech, threats, sharing someone else's private information (including credentials), and repeated unwanted contact are not welcome in project spaces. The maintainer may remove harmful content and restrict participation as needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository owner's profile rather than posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be reviewed confidentially to the extent possible and answered proportionately.

This policy applies to issues, discussions, and other official project spaces.
